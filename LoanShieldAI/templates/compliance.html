RBI Digital Lending Guidelines Reference

Learn about the security guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to protect citizens from illegal digital lending platforms.

Core RBI Directives for Digital Lending

Rule 1 Lending Partner Disclosure

All digital lending apps must disclose the names of their RBI-registered partner NBFCs (Non-Banking Financial Companies) or Banks prominently in the Play Store description and on their website.

Rule 2 Data Access Restriction

Lending apps are strictly prohibited from accessing a borrower's mobile resources such as **Contacts, SMS, Call Logs, and Photos/Storage**. Only one-time access to camera/microphone/location is permitted for KYC verification under strict guidelines.

Rule 3 Key Fact Statement (KFS)

Apps must provide a standardized Key Fact Statement (KFS) to the borrower before loan execution, stating interest rates, Annual Percentage Rate (APR), processing fees, and payment breakdown clearly.

Rule 4 Grievance Redressal

A dedicated Nodal Grievance Redressal Officer must be designated by the lending entity. Their contact details, email address, and physical address must be listed in the app details for customer support.

How to Identify predatory Loan Scam Apps

Short Repayment Tenures: Fake apps usually offer tenures of 7 to 15 days, even if they write "91 days" in the Play Store description. Honest apps must offer minimum 60-90 days repayment tenures.
Free/Google Drive Hosted Policies: Predatory apps often host their privacy policies on free platforms like Google Sites, Blogspot, Google Drive, or Github Pages because they lack official hosting and are created dynamically.
Upfront Processing Fees: Scammers often demand processing charges or security fees *before* approval or disbursement of the loan. Real lenders deduct charges from the disbursed amount and never ask for upfront payments.
Lack of RBI Registration: Always check if the lender claims to partner with an RBI-registered NBFC, and crosscheck that partner's name on the official RBI registry database.
